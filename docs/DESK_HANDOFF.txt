2. Design Principles for 10/10
Desk, not dashboard — One primary focus + clear secondary paths. Never a wall of options.
Curiosity first, progress second — Lead with the mystery of the scenario, then show coverage.
Progress without pressure — Show what is lit without making zeros feel like failure.
One-click depth — From this surface the user should be able to start, resume, or deeply understand the scenario in under two clicks.
Living surface — It should change meaningfully based on user state (first-time, mid-progress, returning after a streak, Portfolio strength, weakest category).

10. Visual & Motion Spec Notes
Maintain the deep green + cream palette (it already feels distinctive and premium).
Chart line should have a very subtle draw-on animation (once).
Orbital coverage visualization: slow, almost imperceptible rotation or breathing when idle.
Primary button: soft elevation + slight scale on hover, clear pressed state.
All interactive elements must have clear focus states for accessibility.
Respect reduced-motion preferences (disable orbital animation and draw-on).
11. Success Criteria for 10/10
A first-time user understands what to do in < 8 seconds.
A returning user can resume or start a high-value next scenario in one click.
Coverage feels motivating rather than incomplete.
The surface never feels like a static poster or a generic dashboard.
Users frequently click into the recommended “next edge”.
The transition from this page into the Interview experience feels seamless and intentional.
