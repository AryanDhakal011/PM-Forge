# PMForge — The Fold

The original mark turns a decision sheet into an F. Its amber corner is the turning point: a small piece of evidence changes direction. The diagonal steps connect brief, evidence and choice.

- `public/brand/pmforge-mark.svg`: master symbol.
- `public/brand/pmforge-logo.svg`: horizontal lockup.
- `public/brand/pmforge-mark-reversed.svg`: light symbol for forest backgrounds.
- `public/brand/pmforge-mark.png`: email-safe PNG.
- `src/components/ui/Logo.tsx`: canonical web component.
- `src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/apple-icon.png`: browser/home-screen icons. The favicon route redirects to the same master mark.

Forest #203d2d, paper #fff8e8, amber #efbd85. Inter 600/500 wordmark; DM Serif Display remains the editorial face. Keep clear space equal to the F stem width (6/40 of the mark). Minimum symbol size: 24 px; navigation: 32 px. No continuous logo animation.

The entrance uses one card unfold and one plane flight, confined to the artwork. Controls remain usable throughout. Optional replay respects OS and saved reduced-motion choices.

Editorial rhythm was informed by [The Vessel](https://vessel-magazine.no/) through an actual Subframe inspiration search. No reference identity or assets were copied.
