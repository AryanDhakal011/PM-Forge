PMFORGE STORYLINE CORRECTIONS HANDOFF (v2.0)
For: the code editor (Claude Code) and the founder. Replaces Section 4 of PMFORGE_PROJECTS_STATUS_AND_CORRECTIONS.txt (the status board stays valid). This document says, level by level, WHAT TO CHANGE IN THE EXISTING STORYLINE to make it more interesting, fun and fruitful. It assumes the shared platform in PMFORGE_PROJECT_PLATFORM_SPEC.txt.
Each correction has its own section with the same layout: A What exists, B Why it falls flat, C Goals, D Scene-by-scene changes (KEEP / CHANGE / ADD / CUT), E New mechanics (exact), F New beats and sample lines, G Receipts and fuses, H Spectacle moments, I Acceptance.
Rules for every correction: lines under 14 words; no more than 3 consecutive non-interactive lines; consequences visible within 2 seconds and again later; every change keeps the learning goal of the level.

======================================================================
0. THE FUN RUBRIC (every level must score 4 of 5 or higher on each; judged in playtests)
======================================================================
 F1 AGENCY: I chose, and the world changed in ways I did not expect.
 F2 SURPRISE: something happened that I had not predicted but could trace to my choices.
 F3 MASTERY: I got better at something in the level (reading evidence, handling a tool, negotiating).
 F4 TENSION AND RELIEF: there were at least three tense moments and three releases.
 F5 DISCOVERY: I found things by looking, asking or exploring, not only by clicking Next.
 F6 TACTILE FEEL: dragging, switching, scrubbing and placing felt physical.
 F7 CHARACTER: I cared what at least two people thought of me.
 F8 HUMOR OR WARMTH: at least two moments made me smile.
 F9 VISIBLE CONSEQUENCE: I could point to a result and say "I did that".
 F10 REPLAY HOOK: I wanted to try a different approach.
Playtest protocol: 6 to 8 players per level; think-aloud; after play, they score F1 to F10 from 1 to 5; any criterion under 4 is fixed before the level is marked ready.

======================================================================
A. WHAT EXISTS (from your walkthrough)
 Entrance with captions or sound; "Meet the team" portrait slideshow (Mara, Dev, Ines); "Understand the case" diagram (Halyard to 12 weeks to Northgate) and resources (12 weeks, 3 investigations, 5 persuasion points, 3 interventions); an 8-stage tracker; Walk the lab with 5 evidence objects (demo footage, incident log, Slack wall, sealed bench report, Northgate letter); Settle three arguments (Speed, Recovery, Learning) with Back Dev, Back Ines, Compromise, Defer; the team reaction screen; the evidence projector with four datasets; the Forge (three controls and a submit loop); five demo events; six endings; a paper conclusion plus an optional trail; replay fork; export.
B. WHY IT FALLS FLAT
 It is a procedure: slideshow, form, projector, form, watch. The big revelations are numbers shown on screens. The "best" build is discoverable from one dataset. Dev and Ines are portraits who read lines; they do not feel like people who want things from you. There is no moment of physical risk, no humor, no personal stake, and consequences arrive in a closing panel.
C. GOALS
 Make the first minute matter; make the lab explorable; make the argument live; keep one ambiguous result; let the player feel the robot; make consequences personal and early; cut about 40% of the reading.
D. SCENE-BY-SCENE CHANGES
 1 Entrance: KEEP. ADD a difficulty choice (Guided, Standard, Sharp).
 2 "Meet the team" slideshow: CUT. REPLACE with a Cold Open (F below): a rehearsal, a near-miss, a decision in 15 seconds. Characters are introduced by what they ask of you.

 4 8-stage tracker: CUT. REPLACE with 3 Acts ("Two Teams", "The Table", "The Demo") and a weeks clock (12 down to 0).
 5 Walk the lab: CHANGE. Make it a free-explore room with three areas and ambient overheard lines when the player lingers near Dev's table or Ines's pit (each gives a hint and nudges nothing). KEEP the 5 evidence objects and the 3-investigation limit. ADD a "second look" (1 attention) on any opened object to reveal a hidden detail (for example the footage shows a second near-miss at 1:52 if you scrub past the marker).
 6 Settle three arguments: CHANGE. From three lanes to a live mediation (E1 below).
 7 Team reaction screen: CHANGE into a hallway scene: two private 1:1 asides (Dev, then Ines); the player chooses the order; the first conversation colors the second.

 9 The Forge: CHANGE. Keep the three controls, but ADD a visible robot (SVG) that changes with each setting and a 15-second Dry Run (seeded wobble) before submit, so the build is felt before the demo.
 10 Submit proposal loop: KEEP the rules (reject, push through at 2 points, conservative build imposed). CHANGE the feedback into spoken, specific objections with a visible "what they mean" chip.

 12 Endings: KEEP the six. ADD per-ending epilogue lines referencing your receipts.
 13 Conclusion: CHANGE. Replace the paper panel by The Replay Wall: three scrubbable moments with your choice highlighted and a "what if" preview for each.

E. NEW MECHANICS
 E1 LIVE MEDIATION (replaces the lanes). For each argument: Dev speaks 2 lines, Ines speaks 2 lines; their faces show a Tension bar; the player has three moves: INTERRUPT (ask for evidence, costs 1 attention), OFFER A THIRD OPTION (a card built from findings), BACK ONE (reduces that lead's resentment, raises the other's). Compromises cost 1 persuasion point and 1 week as before. Defer costs readiness. The same numbers as today drive the outcome; the UI makes them felt.
 E2 PRESS AND HOLD E-STOP (cold open). Hold for 0.4 s to stop the arm; release early and it keeps moving (the arm stops anyway after a beat, but 12 cm from the technician's sleeve). The reading teaches stopping distance by doing.
 E3 DRY RUN. A 15-second simulation after configuring the Forge; shows how the robot behaves with your settings (smooth, jerky, stops, needs help), seeded and reproducible.
 E4 SECOND LOOK. A small attention cost to reveal hidden detail in an opened object; rewards curiosity.

 Humor: TERN-1 chirps in three sounds (happy, worried, sulking); in Shared Ground it bows.
G. RECEIPTS AND FUSES
 Receipts: first reply to Mara's ask; the Speed argument choice and line; the evidence you ignored. Fuses: Dev quotes your Speed decision in the demo; Ines shows the incident slip you skipped; the near-miss at 0:47 returns as event six if not mitigated.
H. SPECTACLE MOMENTS (max 5)

I. ACCEPTANCE
