# The Deadlock: format experiment packet

Status: prepared, not conducted. Use the playable prototype at `/app/projects/deadlock`. The source handoff calls for 6–8 PMs of mixed seniority, 45 minutes each, including at least two mobile users.

Before a session, use a fresh browser profile or start a new run. Explain that the company, people and numbers are fictional. Let the participant choose silent, sound or Listen mode. Ask them to think aloud; provide no coaching. Record screen/audio only with their permission. Use anonymous participant IDs in the attached results sheet.

Observe entry, the three chosen objects, table previews/defenses, projected data order, build settings and proposal responses, interventions, ending and debrief. Note every stall, requested hint, skipped beat, ignored object, use of audio and attempted replay. Count typed characters in Chapters 1–3 separately from the optional debrief note.

After the debrief, ask for the consequence chain they remember without prompting. Collect the two 1–5 ratings (“I felt pressure”; “the room changed because of what I did”), ask whether they prefer this to text case practice, and show one screenshot per chapter for the format-identification test. Offer time to start a fork without directing their choice.

Pass requires at least seven of the handoff's ten criteria:

1. At least 80% finish all three chapters without help.
2. Median pressure rating at least 4/5.
3. Median room-changed-because-of-me rating at least 4/5.
4. At least 70% recall three links in their consequence chain.
5. Zero typed characters during the playable chapters.
6. Median words read per beat at most 40; cinematic skip rate below 50%.
7. At least 50% start a fork replay.
8. At least 90% identify the three chapter formats from screenshots.
9. At least six of eight prefer it to text practice.
10. At most one participant describes it as a form or mostly reading.

Record results in `deadlock-verification/playtest-template.csv`; never replace blank rows with simulated participants. If fewer than seven criteria pass, diagnose the specific scene and retest with three new people before expanding this format to other projects. The balance/audio/art caveats in `DEADLOCK_GAPS.md` remain independent gates.
